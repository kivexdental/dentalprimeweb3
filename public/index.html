More Questions
How often should I visit the dentist?
We recommend visiting your dentist every 6 months for a routine check-up and professional cleaning to maintain optimal oral health and catch any issues early.
Are dental implants painful?
The procedure is performed under local anesthesia so you won't feel pain during surgery. Post-operative discomfort is manageable with prescribed medication.
What is the cost of teeth whitening?
Our laser teeth whitening treatment starts at Rs. 4,500 and can brighten your teeth by several shades in a single visit.
Do you accept insurance?
We accept most major dental insurance plans. We also offer flexible payment plans for larger treatments. Call us before your appointment to verify your coverage.
How do I book an appointment?
You can book online using our patient portal, call us at +91 7802083597, or simply walk-in and generate a token. Same-day appointments available for emergencies.
